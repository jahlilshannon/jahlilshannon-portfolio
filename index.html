AI Feature Risk Triage

Live, open source

Author and maintainer · Public reference framework

Problem
Most AI risk questionnaires produce the same report for every feature. Severity gets asserted rather than derived, and the assessment inherits whatever the submitter claimed.
Solution
A seventeen-dimension questionnaire with a swappable domain profile that defines what severe means before any feature is assessed. Every finding is tagged stated, derived, or unknown. Output is a recommendation with conditions attached, not a verdict.
Outcome
Run two features from the same company through it and it returns opposite answers, which is the only real proof a risk tool discriminates. Mapped to NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and 23894, the OECD classification framework, and the EU AI Act.

Approach

The most useful thing I did last year was cancel something I built

A commitment-tracking tool I had designed and mostly finished went into a stakeholder demonstration and came out cancelled. The demo surfaced data-governance blockers I had missed in discovery. I rebuilt it instead of shipping it.

That is the part of this work I would want to be asked about in an interview, because building AI tooling inside an enterprise is not mostly a building problem. Getting a model to produce something useful is now the easy half. The hard half is knowing what should not ship, and being the person willing to say so about your own work when you are also the one who wants it to launch.

Most AI risk questionnaires produce the same report for every feature. Severity gets asserted rather than derived, and once a rating is in a document it tends to stay there. A tool that flags everything is indistinguishable from a tool that flags nothing.

The risk triage framework is that instinct generalized: force a team to write down what severe means to them before a feature is on the table, separate what was stated from what was derived from what nobody checked, and make the tool capable of saying no with a specific path to yes.

Two other things I hold to. Portability over lock-in: I ported the full delivery-intelligence environment from GitHub Copilot to Claude in two weeks, so leadership has a demonstrated exit path rather than my assurance that one exists. Teaching over building: the measure of the AI skills workstream is not what I shipped, it is that four colleagues shipped their own.
